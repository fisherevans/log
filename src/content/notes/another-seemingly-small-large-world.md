Another seemingly small, but large world editing update - dynamic ambient lighting! Up until now, my lighting layer had a single base color. I added functionality to my map loaded and scene renderer to change the ambient light based on the map default, or a dynamic ambient lighting area, defined with rectangle objects in Tiled. This will allow me to alter the base lighting room by room, or area by area.

In the screen shot below, you can see the rectangles around the temporary dungeon demo areas and the island area.
