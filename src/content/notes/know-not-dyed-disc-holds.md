I know it's not a dyed disc, but it holds 'em! I made this box to charge up my discs while I play glow rounds this winter. The UV lights turn off when the lid is open so it doesn't blind you and the red LEDs are dimmable. It's powered by a rechargable battery and lasts for 5 hours.
