I’m really pleased with this update. Now that I can battle and collect experience and research point, I need a way to spend those things to upgrade the player’s abilities. Since this game has some catch-em-all elements, I couldn’t help but emulate the Pokedex a little bit… Introducing the “Xenolog” - a little PDA that will show your current game progress; and while in HQ, be able to upgrade your player. I’m really happy with this little sub-device menu look and feel. Now I can start on the actual functionality of the menu…
