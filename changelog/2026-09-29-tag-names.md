# 2026-09-29 - One name per tag, links on a tag, and the branch tidied

## What changed

- **`src/lib/tags.ts` is the one place a tag is turned into words.** The metadata
  name if the tag has a yaml file, otherwise the slug title-cased with an acronym
  list (`ui` → UI, `3d-printing` → 3D Printing); a `project:` prefix is structure,
  not a word, so `project:primortal` reads "Primortal". Every chip (stream, post,
  note), both tag pages and the burst cards go through it, and the chip's global
  `text-transform: lowercase` is gone. Slugs appear in URLs only.
- **A tag can carry links.** `links: [{label, url}]` in the tag yaml (also in the
  CMS schema): Primortal points at its projects.fisher.sh page and its repo, LRK at
  its repo. Shown under the tag page header and on the project cards on /tags.
- **The branch under an opened burst** starts under the deck (its back cards reach
  14px below the front, so it used to start from nothing), is centred on the dots
  to the half pixel, and ends where the elbow's straight run ends - the foot below
  the curve is gone. The elbow turns right into a pair of short icon buttons,
  "⌄ 3 more Primortal notes" and "⌃ Hide Primortal notes", whose own background
  covers the line's end.

## Later: the footer and the phone head

- **Burst footer:** a caption ("7 more Primortal notes", or "all 8 Primortal notes
  shown") over two short buttons - bright "⌄ Show 5 more", quiet "⌃ Collapse" - and
  the branch's elbow enters at the buttons' midline (measured equal). The card's
  "All of Primortal →" link is gone: the tag link two lines above it is the same
  thing.
- **Phone head rule:** date left and the three actions as icons right on one row,
  the tag chips on the next. The words stay in the DOM, visually hidden, so screen
  readers still get "Open", "Comment", "Share". Desktop keeps icon + word. A post
  card's action row follows the same rule.
