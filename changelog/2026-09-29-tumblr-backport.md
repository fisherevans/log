# 2026-09-29 - Tumblr Primortal devlog backport

## What changed

- 52 notes tagged `project:primortal` + `gamedev` (+ `pixel-art` where Tumblr had
  `pixelart`), one per post on `fishwingdev.tumblr.com`, 2025-02-12 to 2026-03-05,
  generated by `tools/backport-tumblr.py` from the Tumblr data export on the devbox
  (`/home/dev/social-exports/tumblr`). 46 videos, 15 images, 46 poster frames -
  ~640MB - uploaded to R2 under `notes/YYYY/MM/DD/`.
- Self-hosted `<video>` in a note body now gets the same matte and height cap as a
  YouTube embed in the stream, sized by its own width/height attributes rather than
  assumed 16:9, and a plain full-width treatment on the note page.
- New tag metadata `project-primortal.yaml`.

## Why

The devlog is the densest record of the project and it lived on a platform that is
effectively dormant now. Tumblr stays up as the archive; where new beats get
posted going forward is an open question - dual-posting or back-linking from Tumblr
- but not one this needed answered.

## Decisions

- **Videos carry `preload="none"` and a poster.** 46 videos on one tag page would
  otherwise fetch 46 sets of metadata; with posters the page costs 46 small JPEGs and
  zero video bytes until someone presses play. 14 posts referenced a poster on
  `64.media.tumblr.com` that the export did not include; those posters are frames
  cut from the video with ffmpeg at 1s, so nothing depends on Tumblr staying up.
- **Bodies are converted to markdown, not carried as HTML** - the export uses only
  `p`, `br`, `b`, `i`, `a`, `ul`/`ol`/`li`. Two Tumblr editor artifacts are stripped:
  a bold with no space after it (`**Statuses:**I`, which never closes in
  CommonMark) and a bolded stray first letter (`<b>H</b>ere`).
- **One reblog skipped.** `807943430891323392` is someone else's post with a reply;
  it is theirs, not a devlog beat.
- Dates follow the Instagram convention: local wall-clock time with a `Z` suffix.
