# 2026-09-29 - Instagram disc-dye backport

## What changed

- 58 notes under `disc-dyes` + `disc-golf`, one per Instagram post, 2021-10-23 to
  2023-05-18, generated by `tools/backport-instagram.py` from the Meta data export.
  146 photos and one video uploaded to R2 under `notes/YYYY/MM/DD/<slug>[-n].<ext>`.
- The four hand-seeded dye notes from 2026-08 keep their slug and `id` (comments key
  on it) but now carry their real post dates and the full carousel; `public/seed/` is
  gone.
- Frontmatter `images` now get the Cloudflare width ladder in the stream, the note
  page and the tag gallery. Before this only markdown-body images did, so the dye
  gallery would have shipped 147 full-size 1440px webps.
- The tag gallery is three tiles wide on a phone. `minmax(11rem, 1fr)` collapsed to
  one full-width square per note at 390px, which made the dye page 60,000px tall.

## Why

The dyes were the biggest single block of real content stranded off-site, and the
note is exactly the unit they needed: a photo, a couple of sentences, a date. The
export was already on disk (`/home/dev/social-exports/instagram`, pulled 2026-08-26),
so this was a transform, not a scrape.

## Decisions

- **Dates are the post's local wall-clock time, written with a `Z` suffix.** The site
  treats every date as UTC. Writing `2021-10-23T21:41:32Z` keeps the URL day equal to
  the day it was posted in Vermont and preserves order within a day - fourteen of these
  went up on the same evening.
- **Captions are verbatim** minus the trailing hashtag block; inline hashtags become
  plain words and `@handles` link to Instagram. One typo fixed (`throwbit`).
- **Every post in the export is disc-golf adjacent** (the glow charging box, the dye
  organiser, the photo box, a tournament prize set), so all 58 share the two tags
  rather than a hand-sorted subset.

## Follow-up

The `/posts` stream now has 58 notes in a 19-month run. That is the case the burst
collapsing in the stream is for; it lands next.
