# 2026-09-29 - One share dialog, three actions, a burst that says when

- **Stream actions are Open · Comment · Share.** Link and Share both produced a
  URL and told you so by rewriting their own label for a second; that is two
  buttons for one job and a pattern the site does not use anywhere else. Share now
  opens the same dialog the RSS links use (`LinkModal`): the address in a field,
  Copy, the OS share sheet where the browser has one, Open, Close. Anything on the
  site that hands a reader a link should go through it.
- **Open is an icon + word like its neighbours.** The animated chevron sat further
  from its label than the other icons and moved on hover; nothing else did.
- **A burst card carries a date.** "8 more notes · Feb 11, 2026 · over 4 days" -
  the newest folded note's date, rendered relative ("8 months ago") like every
  other node on the timeline, so a fold between "8 months ago" and "10 months
  ago" is not a hole in time.
- **An opened burst shows five at a time.** "View the notes" reveals five; the
  branch ends in centred controls - "Show 5 more #project:primortal notes · 33
  left" and "Hide these 38 #project:primortal notes" - and the line turns right
  into them instead of curving back to the main spine. A deep link into a folded
  note reveals up to that note. Dots on the branch lose the background halo that
  lifts them off the faint main spine; on an orange line it read as a gap.
- **Headings inside a note body are section labels**, sized just above the prose,
  so a `## Demo` in a note no longer outweighs the post titles around it.
